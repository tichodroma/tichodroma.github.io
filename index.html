Hit the trail in this open-world birdwatching adventure, a labor of love by a former bird biologist turned solo gamedev:

  • Explore pixel habitats across the four seasons, rich with simulated populations.
  • Discover real-world birds through timing-based minigames.
  • Build and maintain a trail network with the Nature Service.
  • Grow your life list and compete in the Birding League.
  • Conduct research become an ornithologist with the Ecology Institute.
  • Deck out your campsite and collect gear to master diverse terrain!
